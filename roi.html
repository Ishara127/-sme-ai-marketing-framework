Research Finding

Why is R² negative? The near-zero and negative R² scores are a substantive research finding — not a model failure. They indicate that structured campaign metrics alone cannot reliably predict SME marketing ROI, because external factors (market sentiment, competitor behaviour, seasonality) dominate outcomes.

What this means for SMEs: Budget allocation decisions should not rely solely on past campaign data. A multi-source AI framework incorporating social listening, competitor tracking, and seasonal signals is required.
